Email assets
============

autoreply.html   Branded auto-response body for the public groups.
autoreply.txt    Plain-text version, and the subject line.

Apply to:  hello@  support@  press@  work@
NOT to:    team@

An internal list must never auto-reply. Two colleagues with
out-of-office enabled will bounce responders off each other until
someone notices, and every member of the group gets a copy of the
noise.

Campaign templates are not here -- they are generated per entry at
/admin/entries/<kind>/<id>/email, so they cannot drift from the
content they announce.
